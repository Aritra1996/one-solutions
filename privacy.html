Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you enter on this website.

Home › Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This policy explains what happens to the information you provide when you use the 1 Solutions website. "We", "us" and "our" refer to 1 Solutions, Andul, Howrah, West Bengal.

What we collect

The only information this website asks for is what you type into the enquiry form: your name, phone number, email address and the service you are interested in. We do not ask for or collect anything else through the site.

How the enquiry form works

When you submit the form, your browser builds a WhatsApp message containing the details you entered and opens WhatsApp so you can send it to us. Nothing is submitted to a server on this website and nothing is stored on this site. The information reaches us only if and when you press send in WhatsApp. Once you do, your message is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

How we use your information

We use the details you send us solely to respond to your enquiry, understand your requirement, provide a quote and deliver the service you ask for. We may contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email in connection with that enquiry.

Sharing

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with third parties for marketing. Information may be shared only where it is necessary to deliver a service you have engaged us for (for example, filing an application with the relevant government authority) or where we are required to do so by law.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set its own cookies and does not run analytics or advertising trackers. Some embedded content, such as the Google Maps panel on our contact page, is served by Google and may set cookies or collect data under Google's own policies when it loads.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts and a public content delivery network, and embeds a Google Map on the contact page. When your browser requests these resources, the providers may receive your IP address and standard request information. We do not control these services.

Retention

Where you become a client, we keep the records needed to provide your service and to meet our own legal and regulatory obligations. Enquiry messages that do not lead to an engagement are retained only for as long as needed to follow up.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit the form and contact us directly by phone or email instead. To ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to request its deletion, contact us using the details below.

Changes
