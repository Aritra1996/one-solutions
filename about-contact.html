Who we are

One partner for your whole back office

1 Solutions is a professional firm offering business registration, tax and compliance, accounting and legal documentation services. Instead of coordinating with a company secretary here, an accountant there and a lawyer somewhere else, our clients work with one team that covers all of it.

We are based in Duillya, Andul Mouri, Howrah, and work with founders, small businesses and established companies across India. Most of the work — consultation, document collection and status updates — happens over WhatsApp, email and phone, so where you are located is rarely a constraint.

  • A single dedicated point of contact for every engagement
  • One all-inclusive quote, agreed before we start
  • End-to-end handling, from paperwork to final filing
  • Ongoing compliance support after registration
